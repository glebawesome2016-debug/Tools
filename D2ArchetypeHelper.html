Start guide: the words this page uses

New to armor stats, or not sure what something on the planner means? Everything is explained here in plain words. When you're ready,

The idea in one sentence: you pick the stats you want, the planner tells you which archetypes to farm (and which random rolls on them), and then you tick pieces off as they drop until all 5 slots are filled.

Archetype

Every legendary armor piece (helmet, gauntlets, chest, legs or class item) has one archetype. It belongs to that single piece, not to your character, your class or the armor set. You'll see the archetype named on the piece when you inspect it in game.

The archetype decides the piece's two biggest stats:

  • Primary stat: 30 points on Tier 5 armor
  • Secondary stat: 25 points on Tier 5 armor

Example: a Gunner helmet always has 30 Weapons and 25 Grenade. There are 12 archetypes, and the table in Step 3 lists them all.

3rd stat

A third, random stat on every piece: 20 points on Tier 5. It's always one of the four stats the archetype doesn't cover, so there's a 1 in 4 chance of each.

When the planner says "20 SP or 20 CL, any of these", a drop with either one works. It moves a mod or tuning elsewhere to make it add up.

Masterwork

Fully upgrading a piece adds +5 to each of the 3 stats left over (the ones that aren't primary, secondary or 3rd stat). That's 15 points, on every tier. The planner always assumes full masterwork.

Tuned stat and tuning mod

Every Tier 5 piece rolls one random tuned stat (it has three small lines next to it in game). It can be any of the 6 stats.

The piece's tuning slot takes one of two mods:

  • +5 / −5: +5 to the tuned stat, −5 to a stat you choose
  • Balanced: +1 to the piece's 3 lowest stats

"Tuned stat to farm: SP" means look for a drop whose tuned stat is Super. "Any" means it doesn't matter.

+10 stat mod

Every piece can hold one +10 stat mod for any stat. The planner places all 5 for you, and you can override one in Your armor if you used something else.

Tier

Armor drops at Tier 1 to 5. Tier 5 has fixed 30 / 25 / 20 stats plus a tuning slot. Tiers 1 to 4 have lower, random stats and no tuning, so results using them are only estimates.

Set mix

How many of each archetype you wear across your 5 pieces. "All 5 the same" is five of one archetype. "3 + 2" is three of one and two of another. "All 5 different" is one each of five archetypes. Mixing is often easier to farm if you already own some pieces.

Fragment bonuses

Some subclass fragments add or remove stat points (for example +10 Class or −10 Health). Enter yours in Step 2 so the totals match what you'll actually see in game.

Target and Priority

A target is the stat total you want (0 to 200). Tick Priority on a stat that must not fall short; if everything can't fit, the planner lets the other stats fall short first.

Armor set

Separate from archetype. A set (like Smoke Jumper) gives a bonus when you wear 2 or 4 pieces of it. Sets don't change stats, so the planner doesn't need them, but you can note each piece's set to track your set bonuses.
